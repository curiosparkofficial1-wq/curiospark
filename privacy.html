Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026

This policy explains how the CurioSpark publishing app ("the app", "we") handles data. The app is a private tool used only by the owner of the CurioSpark channel to publish the channel's own videos. It has no public users and does not access anyone else's accounts.

What the app accesses

  • Google / YouTube (YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API): permission to upload and schedule videos on the owner's channel, update those videos' titles, descriptions, tags and channel branding, and read the channel's basic information and video statistics (for example views and watch time).
  • Meta / Facebook and Instagram (Graph API): permission to publish Reels to the owner's Facebook Page and Instagram professional account, and read basic Page and account information.

The app does not access viewers' personal data, private messages, contacts, or any account other than the owner's.

How data is used

Data is used only to publish and schedule CurioSpark videos and to measure how those videos perform so the owner can improve future videos. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not shared with anyone.

How data is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in the operating system's protected credential store (Windows Credential Manager). Upload records (video IDs, links, publish times) are kept in a local database on the same computer. No data is stored on any CurioSpark server; there is none.

Sharing

We do not share, sell or transfer data to third parties. Data goes only to Google (YouTube) and Meta (Facebook/Instagram), as needed to publish the owner's videos on those platforms.

Google API Services

CurioSpark uses YouTube API Services. Its use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. See also the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • Google: remove CurioSpark's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Meta: remove it in Facebook Settings → Security and login → Business integrations / Apps and websites.

When access is revoked, stored tokens stop working and are deleted from the owner's computer. Local upload records can be deleted on request, and are otherwise kept only as long as needed to avoid duplicate uploads. To ask about or delete data, use the contact below.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from children. CurioSpark videos are family-friendly.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

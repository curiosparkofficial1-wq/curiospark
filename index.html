CurioSpark makes short, family-friendly animated cartoons in everyday Hindi-Urdu that explain the science hiding in daily life: why onions make you cry, why the sky is blue, why you get hiccups. Every fact comes from a trusted source.

About the CurioSpark publishing app

The CurioSpark app is a private tool used only by the CurioSpark channel owner. It uploads and schedules the channel's own videos on the owner's YouTube channel, Facebook Page and Instagram account, and reads basic channel information and statistics for those accounts. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's accounts.

It uses Google's YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API (YouTube API Services), and Meta's Graph API. By using CurioSpark you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
